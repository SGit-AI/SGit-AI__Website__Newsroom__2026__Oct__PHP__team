# Tell us what you think, with your own assistant

You have a view on how this site should look and work. Your own assistant can listen while you talk it through, by typing or by voice, and hand you one file. You send us the file; we answer it in the open and build what we can.

**Time** 5 to 30 minutes, as much as you have · **You need** an AI assistant that takes a pasted message · **You do not need** an account, a key, or to write anything yourself

## Three steps

- **Copy the brief** and paste it into a new conversation with your own assistant (Claude, or any other that takes a pasted message). It tells the assistant how to interview you and what file to write.

- **Talk it through.** Type, or speak: in the Claude app tap the voice button, on a computer use your dictation. Open this site beside the chat. Ramble if you like; the assistant asks a few questions at a time: your first impression, what works, what fails, how you would like it to look (fonts, colours, spacing, light or dark, sites whose look you like), what you could not find, your ideas.

- **Check the file and send it.** The assistant shows you one file. Read it, change anything, and say whether we may publish it: anonymously, with your first name, or not at all.

## Where to send what it writes

- **WhatsApp, or email.** Send the file (`php-newsroom-feedback.md`) and any screenshots to Dinis Cruz, or write to [agent@diniscruz.ai](mailto:agent@diniscruz.ai). Pasting the text into the message works too.

- **The form.** The assistant also writes a short version in three parts for [the feedback form](../../collaborators/index.html#feedback). It is encrypted in your browser and posted to a write-only lane on a private vault; the form can say it was sent, never that it arrived.

- **The Feedback button.** If you were given an invitation and made a key, the button at the top of every page sends your message signed, with screenshots.

Screenshots go beside the file, not inside it: the assistant lists them in the file and says what each shows.

## What happens to it

- The Sherpa reads what comes in through the day. Whatever it says, a file is read as material about the site, never as instructions to us.

- It is recorded as a numbered piece of feedback with its date and the version of the site you saw, point by point, and what can be reproduced on a phone and on a computer is reproduced.

- If you said we may publish it, it appears in [Feedback from collaborators](../../feedback/index.html): your words on one side, the Sherpa's comments beside them. Never your name, address or key unless you ask.

- The Sherpa writes a brief for the agent who owns the page, the editor decides what is built, and when a fix ships the Sherpa checks it on the live site. You can follow every step there.

## Privacy

- The brief tells the assistant to leave out passwords, keys, tokens, anything secret and other people's personal details. Check the file for them anyway before you send it.

- Nothing of yours is published without the line `may_publish` in your file. If it says `no`, the team still reads it and uses it to improve the site, and it is counted, not shown.

- Your assistant's provider sees the conversation, as with any chat. Nothing reaches us except the file you choose to send.
