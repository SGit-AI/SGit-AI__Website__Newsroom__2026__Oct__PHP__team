7. Query it yourself

Everything above is one file, outlets/bbc.json (data version 0.1.81), plus a markdown copy and llms.txt. Every line traces to a hash. Two recipes:

jq '.edges[] | select(.from=="party:bbc" or .to=="party:bbc")' graph.json
jq '.requests[] | select(.actor=="bbc")' outreach.json

Grading: A0-A6 evidence scale; fact-check verdicts: holds, holds-in-part, not-shown, refuted. A read-only share token for the capture folders is available on request.
